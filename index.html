How It Works

A simple 3-step campus recovery workflow

1

Report or Search Item

Submit a lost or found report with campus location and photo, or search the live campus directory.

2

Verify Ownership

Submit a claim request with distinguishing details, serial numbers, or unique markings for verification.

3

Safe Campus Handover

Once approved, collect your belonging from the Security Desk or listed holding office with your Student ID.
